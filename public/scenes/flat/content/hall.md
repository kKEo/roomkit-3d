# Hall

One scene, three rooms. The flat's outline is a plain 36 × 24 m rectangle; two **interior walls** in `scene.json › room.walls` split it into this hall, a gallery and a studio.

Each wall has **openings**: gaps a camera can pass through. The flights between stops find their way through them, and a camera flown by hand (`W` `A` `S` `D`) slides along the walls and can only leave a room by a doorway.
