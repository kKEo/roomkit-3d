# Coffee table

A round top on a central column with a round foot. Type `coffeeTable`: it stands on the floor and is 0.9 m wide, 0.5 m high and 0.9 m deep with its default settings.

| Setting | Type | Default | |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `diameter` | number ≥ 0.4, ≤ 2 | `0.9` | metres across the top |
| `height` | number ≥ 0.25, ≤ 1.1 | `0.45` | metres from the floor to the top surface |

Clickable parts: `top`.
