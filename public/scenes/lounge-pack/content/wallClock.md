# Wall clock

A round clock face on the wall whose minute hand turns slowly. Type `wallClock`: it hangs on a wall and is 0.6 m wide, 0.6 m high and 0.1 m deep with its default settings.

| Setting | Type | Default | |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `diameter` | number ≥ 0.2, ≤ 3 | `0.6` | metres across the face |

It has no clickable parts.
