# Armchair

A low armchair on four short legs. Type `armchair`: it stands on the floor and is 0.9 m wide, 0.9 m high and 0.9 m deep with its default settings.

| Setting | Type | Default | |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `width` | number ≥ 0.6, ≤ 2.4 | `0.9` | metres across, armrests included |

It has no clickable parts.
